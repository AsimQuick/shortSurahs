## Derived Copy Rules

### Strong Signals (4/5+) — We must follow:

| Rule | Tally | Application |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Action verb CTAs | 5/5 | All buttons use imperative verbs: "Begin," "Listen," "Continue," "Play" — never passive nouns |
| Islamic jargon used naturally | 5/5 | Use "surah," "ayah," "Fajr," "Bismillah" without translation. The audience knows these words |
| Imperative CTAs | 5/5 | Direct commands: "Begin Your Journey," "Listen Now," "Sign In" — not questions or suggestions |

### Consider (2-3/5) — Adopt selectively:

| Rule | Tally | Application |
|------|-------|-------------|
| "You/your" language | 3/5 | Use second-person sparingly. "Your journey" feels personal. But some moments should feel universal, not individualistic — "Begin" rather than "Begin your" |
| Spiritual/devotional tone | 3/5 | YES — adopt strongly despite 3/5 tally. Our brand is devotional, not technical. "Come close" over "get started." Quran Majeed's "Come Close to Allah" is the best headline in the competitive set |
| Aspirational/emotional framing | 3/5 | YES — our brand demands this. Features described through spiritual benefit, not technical capability |
| Companion positioning | 3/5 | AVOID. "Companion" implies a relationship with the app. Our app should be invisible — the relationship is between the user and the Quran, not the user and the product |
| Feature-led headlines | 3/5 | AVOID for primary headlines. Use for labels and secondary text only. Primary copy is emotional, not functional |
| Stats/numbers in copy | 3/5 | AVOID. No "X users" or download counts. The app does not need to prove popularity. It needs to feel sacred |
| Sadaqah framing | 2/5 | Not applicable — we're not asking for donations |
| Benefit-led headlines | 2/5 | ADOPT as primary style. Despite low tally, this is the right approach for our emotional brand. "Begin Your Journey" > "8 Surahs Available" |

### Differentiation Opportunities (0-1/5):

| Rule | Tally | Our Opportunity |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Arabic integrated in copy | 1/5 | STRONG differentiator. Only Muslim Pro uses Arabic decoratively in their marketing copy. We integrate Arabic as first-class content throughout — Bismillah headers, Arabic surah names in gold, Arabic prayer names alongside English |
| Short headlines (≤6 words) | 1/5 | ADOPT. Quran Majeed's "Come Close to Allah" (4 words) is the most powerful headline in the set. Our headlines should be brief, reverent, and weighted. Every word earns its place |
| Technology-forward tone | 1/5 | AVOID completely. Only Tarteel uses tech language ("AI powered," "real-time detection"). Our app has no AI, no tech story. The technology is invisible. The experience is sacred |

## Copy Direction for shortSurahs

### Tone
**Sacred minimalism.** Every word is intentional, like calligraphy. The tone is warm but reverent — not casual, not corporate, not technical. Think of how a beautiful mosque feels: welcoming, quiet, unhurried, purposeful.

- NOT casual: No "Hey there!" or "Let's get started!"
- NOT corporate: No "Unlock your potential" or "Optimize your learning"
- NOT technical: No "AI-powered" or "algorithmically curated"
- NOT gamified: No "Keep your streak!" or "You're 40% done!"
- YES reverent: "Begin your journey" / "In the name of Allah"
- YES warm: "Welcome back" / "Continue where you left off"
- YES brief: Every headline ≤6 words. Every label ≤3 words

### Person
Second-person "you" for actions and guidance. No first-person plural "we" (the app doesn't have a personality — it's a vessel). Third-person avoided except for prayer descriptions.

### Headline Formula
```
[Imperative verb] + [spiritual concept]
```
Examples: "Begin Your Journey" / "Continue Your Recitation" / "Know Your Prayers"

NOT:
```
[Adjective] + [feature noun]
```
Anti-examples: "Smart Quran Player" / "Beautiful Recitation App" / "Advanced Prayer Calculator"

### CTA Pattern
```
[Action verb] — 1-2 words max
```
Examples: "Begin" / "Listen" / "Continue" / "Sign In" / "Create Account"

NOT: "Get Started Now" / "Start Your Free Trial" / "Download the App"

### Feature Description Style
Benefit-through-experience, not feature-list:
- YES: "Listen to each ayah until it's part of you"
- NO: "Loop audio playback with per-ayah controls"
- YES: "Every surah, every ayah — even offline"
- NO: "Offline audio support with bundled assets"

### Labels and Metadata
Minimal, functional, no decoration:
- "4 Ayahs" not "4 beautiful ayahs"
- "Meccan" not "Revealed in Mecca"
- "Next: Asr · 3:45 PM" not "Your next prayer is Asr at 3:45 PM"

### Forbidden Copy Patterns
- No exclamation marks (ever — the Quran doesn't shout)
- No emoji (explicit PRD constraint)
- No ellipsis for mystery/intrigue ("Discover what awaits...")
- No superlatives ("the best," "the most beautiful," "the #1")
- No comparison to competitors ("unlike other apps")
- No urgency language ("Don't miss out," "Limited time," "Act now")
- No gamification language ("streak," "badge," "achievement," "level up," "progress")
- No tech jargon ("algorithm," "AI," "machine learning," "optimized")
- No placeholder/lorem ipsum — every string must be final copy

## Example Copy Bank for shortSurahs

These are directional examples to guide Phase 2+ copy writing. Final copy will be written per-section.

### Login / Sign-Up Screen
- Header: "بِسْمِ ٱللَّٰهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ" (Bismillah)
- Subheader: "Begin Your Journey"
- Email field label: "Email"
- Password field label: "Password"
- Primary CTA: "Create Account" / "Sign In"
- Toggle: "Already have an account? Sign In" / "New here? Create Account"
- Back to social sign-in: "Other sign-in options"

### Home / Surah List
- Section label: "Short Surahs"
- Arabic subtitle: "سور قصيرة"
- Welcome (first run): "Begin with any surah"
- Next prayer: "Next: Asr · 3:45 PM"
- Surah card: "Al-Ikhlas" / "ٱلْإِخْلَاص" / "Sincerity" / "4 Ayahs"

### Now-Playing Bar
- Format: "Al-Ikhlas · Ayah 2 of 4"
- No "Now Playing" label — the bar's existence IS the label

### Prayer Times
- Screen title: "Prayer Times"
- Arabic: "أوقات الصلاة"
- Each prayer: "Fajr · الفجر · 5:12 AM"
- Next prayer indicator: subtle, not text-heavy — visual emphasis only

### Account
- Screen title: "Account"
- Location: "Prayer Location"
- Action: "Update Location"
- Trust text: "Used for prayer times only"
- Sign out: "Sign Out"
